\opening{To the Android Team:} 
 
\noindent I'd like to pursue a career with you with opportunity for growth.

This lead me to study computer science as a software engineer with a special focus on mobile applications.
Wanting to share my passion with others, I developed mobile programming courses at Florida State University geared towards application development for Windows 8, Windows Phone 8, and Android.
These courses were so popular, they were adopted into the curriculum.

However, it's time for me to leave academia because I need the chance to shape this future.
