I'm writing to apply for the Director, AI Enablement \& Strategy role within Disney Experiences Technology. For the past six years I've built the exact program this role describes -- AI operating models, value dashboards and ROI frameworks, governance, and culture change toward AI-native teams -- first at Microsoft for enterprise and Fortune 500 customers, and more recently leading it directly as a Principal Cloud Solution Architect. I'd like to bring that experience to help lead AI augmentation across DXT.

\textbf{Why Disney.} I grew up a Disney kid in Florida, long before I had a name for it -- my family held season passes, and one stretch we spent 52 straight days on Disney property while camping nearby. That feeling never left. I proposed to my wife at Cinderella's wishing well, and we later moved back from Redmond, Washington to Florida in part to be closer to family and to the parks. Everything changed again in 2023: holding my daughter in her Winnie the Pooh nursery, I broke down in tears and told my wife, let's renew our passes. Watching her talk to Mickey like an old friend and sing with the princesses is the closest thing I have to a definition of magic. As I've gotten older, I've come to prioritize memories and family time over everything -- and few things have made their value to me clearer than watching my daughter experience Disney exactly the way I once did.

\textbf{Why this role.} What draws me most here isn't the technical challenge -- it's the responsibility. Getting to help steward the Disney brand, and protect what makes it work as AI reshapes how DXT builds, feels less like a job and more like an honor. Building the governance and platform that let delightful, trustworthy experiences reach families safely is exactly what I want my career to be about. I've spent recent years leading AI governance and adoption for large financial services platforms -- important work, but work that never quite reached me the way this does. As I get older, purpose matters more to me than it used to, and this role feels like wind in my sails: a chance to finally point everything I've built toward something I love.

I'd welcome the chance to talk through how this experience maps to DXT's AI augmentation roadmap.
