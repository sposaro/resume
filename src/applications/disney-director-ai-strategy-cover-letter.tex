Dear Disney Experiences Technology Hiring Team,

\vspace{2pt}
I've spent 20+ years in enterprise technology -- the last six building the exact program this role describes, working across dozens of enterprises' AI journeys in a way most internal teams haven't had the chance to. But that's not why I'm writing this letter.

\textbf{Why Disney.} I grew up a Disney kid in Florida, long before I had a name for it -- my family held season passes, and one stretch we spent 52 straight days on Disney property while camping nearby. That feeling never left. I proposed to my wife at Cinderella's wishing well, and we later moved back from Redmond, Washington to Florida in part to be closer to family and to the parks. Everything changed again in 2023: holding my daughter in her Winnie the Pooh nursery, I broke down in tears and told my wife, let's renew our passes. Watching her talk to Mickey like an old friend and sing with the princesses is the closest thing I have to a definition of magic. As I've gotten older, I've come to prioritize memories and family time over everything -- and few things have made their value to me clearer than watching my daughter experience Disney exactly the way I once did.

\textbf{Why this role.} What draws me most here isn't the technical challenge -- it's the responsibility. Getting to help steward the Disney brand, and protect what makes it work as AI reshapes how DXT builds, feels less like a job and more like an honor. Building the governance and platform that let delightful, trustworthy experiences reach families safely is exactly what I want my career to be about. I've spent recent years leading AI governance and adoption for large financial services platforms -- important work, but work that never quite reached me the way this does. As I get older, purpose matters more to me than it used to, and this role feels like a chance to finally point everything I've built toward something I love.

There's nothing I'd rather build than the kind of AI foundation that lets that magic reach the next generation of families safely and reliably -- starting, maybe, with my daughter's.
